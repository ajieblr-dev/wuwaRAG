RESONANCE ARCHIVE / ABOUT

A grounded lore archive across Solaris-3, wired to the wiki.

Resonance Archive is an Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation platform over the Wuthering Waves wiki. It indexes all Main Quests from Prologue through Chapter IV and delivers grounded answers backed by verified dialogue passages.

CORPUS SCOPE

161 lore & quest pages, 4,323 chunks, 1,119 speakers

The knowledge base covers the full storyline from the Prologue: Utterance of Marvels through Chapter I, II, III and Chapter IV, plus key world lore hubs. Dialogue turns and cutscenes are parsed with preserved speaker identities and quest objectives.

HOW IT WORKS

Wiki to answer: the hybrid retrieval pipeline

01 Fandom wiki MediaWiki API
02 Parser & chunker dialogue + speaker tags
03 Dense + keyword 384-dim + heuristics
04 Hybrid RRF Qdrant · k=60
05 LiteLLM gateway Gemini / Groq + history

The answer step is orchestrated by LiteLLM (wuwa-lore-model), dynamically routing across Gemini 3.5 Flash and Groq Llama 3.3 70B, with intelligent query rewriting that maintains conversation context across follow-up questions.
