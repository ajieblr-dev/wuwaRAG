Resonance Archive / about

A grounded lore archive across Solaris-3, wired to the wiki.

Resonance Archive is an Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platform over the Wuthering Waves wiki. It indexes all Main Quests from Prologue through Chapter IV (Huanglong, Mt. Firmament, Black Shores, and Rinascita) and delivers grounded answers backed by verified dialogue passages.

Corpus scope

78 Quest Pages, 3,786 Chunks, 1,119 Dialogue Speakers

The knowledge base covers the full storyline from the Prologue: Utterance of Marvels through Chapter I, II, III, and Chapter IV (Rinascita & Septimont), plus key world lore hubs. Dialogue turns and cutscenes are parsed with preserved speaker identities and quest objectives.

Prologue: Utterance of Marvels Huanglong

Awakening of the Rover, encountering Yangyang and Chixia, Gorge of Spirits, and arrival at the border of Jinzhou.

Chapter I: Huanglong & Jinzhou Acts I – VII

Rewinding Raindrops, Grand Warstorm, Clashing Blades, Norfall Barrens campaign against the Threnodian Ovathrax.

Chapter II: Mt. Firmament Acts I – II

Thaw of Eons, Truthseeker's Pass, Sentinel Jue, temporal anomalies, and the history of Hongzhen.

Chapter III: The Black Shores Acts I – II

To the Shore's End, Song of the Heart Sword, Tethys System, and the tragic origin of the Shorekeeper.

Chapter IV: Rinascita & Septimont Acts I – V

The Maiden, The Defier, The Death Crier, By Sun's Burning Hand, and the confrontation with the Sentinel of Rinascita.

Lore Hubs & Major Arcs World Lore

Simulacrum of the Heart (Version 3.7+), Sentinel Hsin, Intendant Suoming, Mengzhou, and Xuanfang Hold.
