Resonance Archive / about

An observatory for one arc, wired to the wiki.

Resonance Archive is an unofficial, self-hosted Retrieval-Augmented Generation service. It answers lore questions from a small, fixed slice of the Wuthering Waves wiki and shows you the passages it used.

Corpus scope

Five pages, 222 chunks, 71 speakers

The index covers the main quest Simulacrum of the Heart (Version 3.7+). Anything outside this set returns a thin answer — the service prefers an honest gap over invention.

The answer step is routed through a LiteLLM alias (wuwa-lore-model), which may fall back from a primary model to a lighter one, or degrade to retrieval-only when the gateway is unavailable.

Privacy

Where your question goes

Your question is sent to this app's API, and — together with the retrieved passages — forwarded to external LLM providers through the gateway. Question history is stored only in your own browser.
