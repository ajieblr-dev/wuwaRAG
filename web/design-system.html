Resonance Archive / design-system

Design system

Every token, component, and state used across the reader and the console — rendered live in both themes. Concept: a Tacet resonance codex meets an editorial lore archive.

Color tokens

Dark Tacet abyss is the default. Resonance teal is the signal color; brass is reserved for citations and key lore callouts. Values are the only hex literals in the codebase — components reference tokens only.
